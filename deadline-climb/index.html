    How to play

    1. Walk with ← → (or A D). Stand on a ladder and press ↑ ↓ (or W S) to climb.
    2. Ladders with a 🔒 are locked. Press ↑ at one to get a headline puzzle: tap the misspelled word, or tap words in order to rebuild a real DN headline. Solving it unlocks every ladder on that floor for +300. The press pauses while you think, and a wrong tap costs 50.
    3. Press Space to jump over the paper rolls tumbling down from the press. Each roll you clear is worth 100.
    4. Grab the floating newspapers for 250 each. Every one is a real Daily Nebraskan story about elections, ballot initiatives or campus politics.
    5. Reach the top floor and touch the red STOP button to finish the edition. Get there fast to keep more of the bonus.
    6. You have 3 lives. Each new edition makes the press a little faster.

    On a phone, use the arrow pad and the big JUMP button under the game.
