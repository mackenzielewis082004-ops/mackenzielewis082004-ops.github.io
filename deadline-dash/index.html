    How to play

    1. Steer your reporter (the dark square) with ←↑↓→ or WASD. On a phone, swipe or use the arrow pad.
    2. Run over a story tip to file it. Each one is a real, recent Daily Nebraskan headline.
    3. Every story adds time to your deadline clock and grows your column of copy behind you.
    4. Grab tips from the section your editor wants for 3× points and extra time. The assignment changes every few stories.
    5. Your edition goes to print — game over — if the clock hits zero, or you crash into the wall or your own column.
