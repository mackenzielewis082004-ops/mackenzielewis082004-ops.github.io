  • Inverted pyramid: most important information first, supporting details after.
  • Every factual claim needs attribution. Who said it? How do they know?
  • The lede should answer who, what, when, where — why and how can follow.
  • Verify names, titles and numbers with a second source before publishing.
  • AP style: spell out numbers one through nine; use figures for 10 and up.
  • Headlines use present tense for past events.
