Headline Scramble

Our copy desk dropped this week’s headlines and the words went everywhere. Can you put them back in order?

How to play
  • Every puzzle is a real headline from the Daily Nebraskan, with its words shuffled.
  • Tap the words in order to rebuild the headline. Tap a word you placed to send it back.
  • When every word is placed, the game checks your answer and tells you how many words are in the right spot.
  • Stuck? Hint locks in the next correct word (−25 points). Solve faster to earn more points.
  • Solve headlines in a row without hints to build a 🔥 streak. Each one adds +50 bonus points.
  • There are 6 headlines per game. When you solve one, you can click through to read the real story.
