How to play

  1. Real Daily Nebraskan headlines slide down the copy desk toward the press.
  2. Some of them have a typo: swapped letters, a missing letter, a doubled letter or the wrong vowel. Click or tap that word to fix it.
  3. Each fix is worth 3 points × your streak. Every fix in a row raises your streak, up to ×5.
  4. Clean headlines that reach the press earn 1 point. Click a word that's spelled correctly and you lose 1 point and your streak.
  5. If a typo gets printed, the paper has to run a correction. You can run five corrections before your shift is over.
  6. The press starts slow and speeds up a little the longer you last.

Copy desk ranks

  • 0–19: Intern
  • 20–59: Copy Editor
  • 60–119: Chief Copy Editor
  • 120–199: Managing Editor
  • 200+: AP Stylebook in human form

Real stories

Every headline is a real story from dailynebraskan.com. The typos are made up by the game. Your end-of-shift report links to every story you handled.
